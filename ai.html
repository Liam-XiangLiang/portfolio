Systematic Trading & Behavioral Finance Terminal

An enterprise-grade desktop application 100% solo-developed via AI-driven workflows, engineered to streamline complex quantitative trading and behavioral analysis. The system integrates real-time price feeds via advanced APIs, multi-asset screening tables, and dynamic charting canvases. To optimize high-pressure execution, it features a structured pipeline spanning pre-trade risk evaluation, rule-based strategy compliance, and post-trade cognitive review. Innovation loops include an automated OCR ingestion engine for 1-second visual logging, an experimental volatility matrix mapping classical 'I Ching' paradigms to market cycles, option pricing models, and an embedded micro-music client to cultivate psychological 'flow states' during active market sessions.

Quantitative UI/UX Computer Vision (OCR) Behavioral Analytics

Multi-Dimensional Language Cockpit

A custom-built vocabulary learning tool that replaces isolated flashcard memorization with a connected knowledge system. Words are automatically clustered by shared roots, prefixes/suffixes, semantic tags, and multilingual definitions, so learners see how vocabulary relates instead of memorizing terms in isolation. Full keyboard-hotkey and mobile touch-gesture support keeps review sessions fast and interruption-free.

Linguistic Architecture Human-Computer Interaction Data Persistency

Immersive Bible Reader

A dual-purpose application focused on typographic excellence. Beyond a distraction-free dark mode reading experience, it features a rapid-export engine that instantly compiles users' favorite verses into beautifully typeset, high-resolution mobile wallpapers for daily motivation.

Minimalist Music Player

A lightweight local music client built as a reaction against bloated, feature-heavy media players. Essential playback controls live in a compact, collapsible UI that stays out of the way — built for creative professionals who want music running in the background without breaking focus.
